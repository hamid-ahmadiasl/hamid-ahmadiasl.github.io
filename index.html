Profile summary

AI and computational science researcher with a mechanical engineering background, currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Science and Technology at the University of Milano-Bicocca. I build machine-learning models for physical and engineering systems, using CFD and experimental data to train surrogate models, reconstruct flow fields and explore design spaces.

My work sits where physics meets data: scientific machine learning, the data quality and generalization of small-sample surrogates, and data-driven design optimization. Years of R&D and industrial experience as a Project Sub-Leader (PSL) and Design Engineer, together with patents and peer-reviewed publications, keep my models grounded in what real engineering problems need.

I'm driven by research that uses AI to accelerate scientific discovery and engineering design, and I regularly share training posts that explain ML and deep learning concepts.
